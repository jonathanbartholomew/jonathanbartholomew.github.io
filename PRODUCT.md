# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential Daedabyte clients evaluating Jonathan Bartholomew for a senior full-stack role or contract engagement. They're skimming quickly — scanning for signal of real seniority (HIPAA/healthcare systems, AI tooling, shipped products) and craft (the site itself is a work sample).

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that makes the case for hiring or contracting Jonathan directly through the quality of its execution, not just its content. Success looks like a visitor walking away convinced he's a senior engineer who ships — informed by the same real experience, education, and shipped work a resume would carry, but presented with more taste than a resume ever could.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, confident, precise. Stark black background, single high-contrast accent (burnt orange/red), oversized condensed display type (Bebas Neue) paired with a restrained body sans (DM Sans). Editorial and technical at once — more architecture-monograph than SaaS-marketing-site.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS/template portfolio patterns: identical card grids, gradient text, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, hero-metric-with-gradient-accent clichés, glassmorphism-as-decoration, side-stripe borders. Should not read as "built from a portfolio template."

## Design Principles

- Craft is the pitch — the site's own execution is evidence of the seniority being claimed.
- Real information over decoration — every stat, date, and project link should be true and verifiable, not filler.
- One accent color, used with intent, not sprinkled.
- Big typography carries hierarchy; avoid leaning on cards/borders/eyebrows to fake structure.
- Motion should feel deliberate (GSAP-driven reveals, magnetic CTAs) and never gratuitous.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline: sufficient color contrast, `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback for all animation, keyboard-navigable nav/links/form.
